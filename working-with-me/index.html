03 · One-Off Payment

Two ways to handle payment.

Option A

Pay the full estimated amount upfront

If I estimate a task at 3 hours, the expected fee is:

3 × USD 15 = USD 45

If the agreed scope does not change, but the task takes longer because my estimate was wrong, you do not pay extra for my estimating mistake.

If you trust my estimate enough to pay the full amount upfront, I should take responsibility for that estimate.

Option B

Pay 50% of the estimated amount first

If a 3-hour task is estimated at USD 45, the initial payment is:

USD 45 × 50% = USD 22.50

With this option, the final fee is based on actual working time.

If the task ultimately takes 5 hours, the total is USD 75. After deducting the USD 22.50 deposit, the remaining balance is USD 52.50.

Final files, source code or other complete deliverables are released after the remaining balance is paid.

Trust works both ways.

06 · Why This Model

Why can this make sense for a small business?

These are not equivalent options. The real question is whether your business has enough continuous technical work to justify a full-time position.

Full-Time Employee

Full-time technical role

  • Fixed salary and long-term employment costs
  • Makes sense when there is steady, continuous work
  • The cost remains even during quieter periods
  • Better suited to businesses with high-frequency technical needs
One-Off Freelancer

Find someone each time

  • Search for the right person again
  • Re-explain your files, workflow and business context
  • Different people may work and deliver in different ways
  • Useful for truly occasional, isolated tasks
Monthly Technical Support

Keep one technical contact

  • USD 300 per month for up to 40 task hours
  • Data, automation, Excel, lightweight tools and system-data work can share the same hours
  • The same person gradually learns your tools, files and workflow
  • Designed for technical work that is recurring but not enough to justify a full-time role
Monthly support is not a replacement for a full-time employee. It is for businesses that have recurring technical work, but not necessarily enough of it to justify a full-time technical position.

08 · Time Tracking

I charge for doing your work, not for learning something new.

Task time is based on the actual time used to complete your work. The minimum time unit is 10 minutes.

Analysis, processing, development, testing and delivery that are directly related to your task count as task time.

Pure waiting time, my own general learning time, and rework caused by my own mistakes are not charged to your task hours.

You are paying for the time I spend completing your work, not for the time I spend learning something new.

10 · Unused Hours

Unused hours do not roll over.

The 40 task hours belong to the current monthly plan period and do not automatically carry forward into the next month.

The monthly plan is not only about buying a block of hours. It also keeps a stable technical-support point of contact available when something comes up.

You may not use all 40 hours every month. The real value is that when the next problem appears, you do not need to start searching for someone capable of solving it all over again.

15 · After-Sales Support

Different types of work need different support.

One-off clients and monthly clients are not treated the same way, because they are buying different kinds of working relationships.

Monthly Clients

Ongoing support stays ongoing.

Normal adjustments, maintenance, new requests and changes caused by third-party platforms can be handled within the monthly service and use monthly task hours.

Fixing my own delivery mistakes or bugs does not consume your monthly task hours.

One-Off Clients

Clear support periods after delivery.

For data-processing tasks, if the delivered result does not match the agreed processing rules, I will correct it free of charge within 5 working days after full delivery, provided the requirements have not changed.

For functional deliverables such as Excel automation, Python tools, workflows, app integrations, Google Apps Script, browser extensions and lightweight desktop tools, I provide a 22-working-day defect-fix period after full delivery.

New requirements, customer-side environment changes, customer changes to data structure or configuration, and third-party platform changes are not treated as defects in the original delivery.

Fixing my mistake should not cost you again. New requirements and new external changes are new work.
